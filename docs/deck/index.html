The problem
02

Trust in travel is bought, and loyalty costs your data.

Reviews anyone can buy

Platforms show thousands of stars and none of them prove a stay. A guest, or the guest's AI agent, cannot tell a real review from a paid one.

Loyalty paid for with your data

A returning-guest discount means handing over your identity and your history. You pay for the perk with a profile that follows you everywhere.

Agents now shop for people. They need context they can verify, and people need it to stay theirs.

BookerBobproblem
The solution
03

Prove you were a customer. Reveal nothing else.

One zero-knowledge membership proof, verified on Stellar. Only its scope changes between uses.

01 reviews

reviews nobody can buy

perks that stack, privately

Returning guest 10%, BookerBob network 5%, REP verified traveler 5%, capped at 15%. One USDC payment.

03 agent checkout
