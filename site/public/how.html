How it works

Agents rank on proofs nobody can fake, and add new ones every time they buy.

BookerBob is Google for agentic commerce. Places, stores and travelers carry proven context; an agent ranks on it, buys with it, and every purchase becomes a new proof. Nobody learns who you are.

The core loop

Every purchase makes the next ranking better.

The engine

Four primitives.

Groups

Who proved what: a place's customers, a network, verified travelers, wallet tiers. Only anonymous commitments.

Proofs

A zero-knowledge proof that you are in a group, checked by Stellar. Which member stays secret.

Perks

Each place sets what each group earns, with a cap. Perks stack and settle in one payment.

Attesters

Anyone who can vouch adds a group: REP, Stellar wallet tier, the BookerBob network, and more.
